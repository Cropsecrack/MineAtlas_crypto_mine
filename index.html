05 / Before you buy

A practical verification checklist

Take time to verify the exact item and seller—especially for expensive hardware and self-custody products.

01 — SOURCE

Verify the seller

Find the manufacturer’s current authorized-reseller information independently. Check business registration, support history, return terms, and warranty details.

02 — MODEL

Confirm exact specifications

Match the precise model and revision against official product documentation. Check power draw, electrical connector, cooling, noise, and environmental requirements.

03 — SECURITY

Protect your keys

Set up wallets using official instructions. Never enter a recovery phrase into a website, send it to support, or accept a pre-generated recovery phrase.
