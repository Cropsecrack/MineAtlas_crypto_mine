07 / Verification guide

Before you buy

Use a structured verification process for expensive hardware and self-custody products.

01 — SOURCE

Verify the seller

Use independently discovered manufacturer or authorized-reseller information. Check business details, support history, returns, and warranty.

02 — MODEL

Confirm exact specifications

Match the revision against official documentation. Check power, connectors, cooling, noise, environment, and service requirements.

03 — SECURITY

Protect your keys

Initialize wallets using official instructions. Never enter a recovery phrase into a website or send it to support.
