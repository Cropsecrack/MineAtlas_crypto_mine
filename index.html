Quick answers

Common questions

Need a quick answer? Open the chat button in the corner to ask the built-in equipment helper.

Does this page guarantee mining profits?

No. Mining outcomes depend on many changing factors. This page does not provide income predictions or financial advice.

Why does the video start muted?

Browsers commonly restrict autoplay with sound. The embedded clips start muted; playback behavior may vary by browser and device.

Does the calculator include cooling costs?

No. It estimates the electricity used by the wattage you enter. Add cooling and other loads separately to your own calculation.

Is the chatbot connected to a service?

No. It gives preset answers in this browser page. Messages are not sent to a server or saved by the chatbot.
