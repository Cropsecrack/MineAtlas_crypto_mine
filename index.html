Why mining, wallets & crypto matter

A short, original animated explainer about network participation, self-custody, and responsible research.

Chapter 1 · The network
⛏

A network needs participants.

Mining devices compete to add valid blocks to proof-of-work networks. This work helps order transactions and makes tampering costly—but it uses energy and does not guarantee a reward.

00:00 / 01:30
Read the explainer transcript

Crypto networks are shared systems that use rules to record and verify digital transactions. In proof-of-work networks such as Bitcoin, miners use specialized hardware to compete to propose valid blocks. Mining helps secure transaction history, but requires substantial electricity, careful infrastructure planning, and carries financial and operational risks. A hardware wallet helps keep signing keys under your control; it does not store coins themselves. Protect the recovery backup and never share it. Learn how the technology works, verify claims through primary sources, understand local rules and energy costs, and never treat mining or crypto as guaranteed income.
